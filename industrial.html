Industrial Capabilities

Integrated technical services designed to improve performance, reduce risk and support safe, compliant and reliable operations.

Capital Project Management

Project development and execution support from initial concept, business justification and scope definition through procurement, installation, commissioning and operational turnover.

Equipment Installation & Commissioning

Coordination of equipment receiving, rigging, mechanical installation, utility connections, electrical work, controls integration, startup, punch-list completion and system readiness.

Sterilization Systems

Engineering and project support for Ethylene Oxide and steam sterilization systems, including Vacudyne, Getinge and Trumbo equipment.

Validation & Qualification

Development and execution of User Requirements Specifications, validation strategies, remediation protocols, IQ/OQ/PQ protocols, test documentation and qualification reports.

Sterilization Remediation

Technical assessment, troubleshooting, protocol development, validation execution and corrective-action support for EtO sterilization equipment, controls and associated systems.

Automation & Control Systems

Support for sterilization and process control systems, including Allen-Bradley and Siemens PLC platforms, monitoring, tracking, instrumentation and system modernization.

Facilities & Critical Utilities

Engineering support for HVAC, boilers, steam, chilled water, compressed air, vacuum systems, scrubbers, heat exchangers, water systems and electrical infrastructure.

Reliability Engineering

Asset reliability assessments, failure analysis, equipment criticality, improvement strategies and technical recommendations to reduce downtime and extend asset life.

Preventive & Predictive Maintenance

Development and optimization of maintenance programs based on equipment criticality, failure history, operating requirements and long-term reliability objectives.

Clean Rooms & Controlled Environments

Technical coordination for clean-room construction, facility upgrades, HVAC requirements, utilities integration and project execution within controlled manufacturing environments.

Contractor & Vendor Coordination

Management and coordination of electricians, pipefitters, welders, mechanical contractors, automation specialists, insulation installers and other technical service providers.

Technical Consulting

Engineering guidance for troubleshooting, CAPA support, system improvements, facility upgrades, project recovery, operational readiness and technical decision-making.

Our Project Approach

A structured process that connects engineering, execution, commissioning and qualification.

01

Assess

We evaluate existing conditions, process requirements, equipment, utilities, risks, compliance needs and operational priorities.

02

Define

We establish user requirements, project scope, design criteria, resources, schedule, responsibilities and execution strategy.

03

Execute

We coordinate procurement, contractors, installation, utilities, controls, documentation, safety and site activities.

04

Commission

We verify installation, controls, functionality, alarms, utilities, operational sequences and equipment readiness.

05

Qualify

We develop or execute IQ/OQ/PQ protocols, document results, address deviations and support validated operational handover.

06

Improve

We identify opportunities to increase reliability, capacity, efficiency, compliance and long-term asset performance.
