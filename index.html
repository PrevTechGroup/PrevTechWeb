About PrevTech Group

PrevTech Group LLC is a Puerto Rican firm dedicated to delivering integrated technical solutions for regulated sectors, maritime industries, and SMEs. We specialize in industrial maintenance, project management, and sustainable energy solutions, ensuring operational excellence, safety, and compliance. Through our specialized divisions—PrevTech Main, PrevTech Projects, PrevTech Steril, and PendTech Marine—we provide scalable services tailored to each client’s needs. Our commitment to technical excellence and strategic integration drives us to create reliable, efficient, and future-ready solutions

At PrevTech Group LLC, our team is composed of highly skilled professionals and certified specialists dedicated to delivering technical excellence. We combine engineering expertise, regulatory knowledge, and hands-on experience to provide integrated solutions for complex industrial, commercial, and maritime environments.
